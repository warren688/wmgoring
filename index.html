Disclaimer

Please read the following carefully before using this site or riding any of the trail networks featured.

Liability Disclaimer

Neither Warren & Melissa Go Riding nor anyone associated with Warren & Melissa Go Riding will be responsible or liable for any injury sustained while riding the trail networks we film.

Medical Advice

Consult a doctor before starting any exercise program. This applies to all riders regardless of age or fitness level.

Extreme Sport Warning

Mountain biking is an extreme sport. Riders should be aware of the dangers at all times. Failure to take appropriate precautions may result in serious injury or death.

Ride Smart

Always ride within your limits and in groups where possible. Never ride alone on remote trails. Know your ability and choose trails accordingly.

Working Farms

Many trails featured on this site are situated on working farms. Trail conditions are subject to change without notice or indication. Always check current conditions before visiting a hub.

By using this site and watching our content you acknowledge that you have read and understood this disclaimer. Ride safe, have fun, and look out for each other out there.

Why We Exist

Mountain Biking
Has the Power to Change
Lives.

It gets us outside, challenges us, introduces us to incredible people, and takes us to places we'd never discover any other way. That's why WMGoriding exists.

Our Mission

Get More People Riding

To help more people get on their bikes and experience the incredible trails and natural beauty South Africa has to offer. Completely free. No paywalls, no subscriptions.

Our Goal

SA's Most Trusted MTB Resource

Not by telling people where they have to ride, but by giving them the information they need to choose the rides that are right for them. Whether it's your first trail or your next big adventure.

How We Measure Success

"I Can Ride There."

Not views or subscribers. Success is measured by the number of people who finish watching one of our videos and think: "I can ride there." That's it. That's the whole thing.

The Riders

Warren
& Melissa

Cape Town · Western Cape

Like many riders, we've stood in a parking lot wondering: which trail should we ride? Is it too technical? Do I need a permit? Where do I park? Can beginners ride here? Is it worth the drive?

Those questions inspired WMGoriding. We realised that finding honest, practical information about South African mountain bike trails wasn't always easy. So we started documenting our rides, not to show how fast we could ride, but to help other riders feel confident enough to experience these amazing places for themselves.

Today, WMGoriding is a growing library of trail guides, route information, local knowledge and real riding experiences. We're proud to work with trail builders, landowners, conservation organisations and brands who share our passion for growing mountain biking and protecting the incredible places we ride.

Every great ride starts long before your tyres touch the dirt. It starts with confidence. It starts with good information. It starts with the excitement of discovering somewhere new.

Plan Your Ride

Weekend
Plans

Not just a trail. A full weekend. Where to ride, where to eat, where to stay and what to do in between. Warren and Melissa's favourite Western Cape MTB weekends, planned for you.

Weekend Plan 1

The Perfect
Stellenbosch Weekend

Two of the finest trail hubs in the Winelands, world-class food and wine, and a base in one of South Africa's most beautiful towns. This is the weekend that converts people to mountain biking for life.

📍 Stellenbosch 🚵 Intermediate 📅 2 Days

Friday

Drive to Stellenbosch. Check in to your accommodation. Dinner at De Warenmarkt or Terroir. Early night — you have a big day tomorrow.

Saturday

Early start at . Ride the Armageddon Trail. Permits R90 at the gate. Balance Coffee at the entrance for a pre-ride fix. Post-ride lunch in Stellenbosch town. Afternoon wine tasting at Muratie or Tokara. Braai or dinner at Jardine.

Sunday

Morning ride at . Some of the best singletrack in the country in a stunning valley setting. Lunch at Babylonstoren on the way home. Back in Cape Town by late afternoon.

Stay

Stellenbosch town has everything from budget guesthouses to boutique hotels. Oude Werf Hotel is a favourite. Alternatively, several Jonkershoek Valley farm stays put you right at the trailhead.

Weekend Plan 2

The Winelands
Big Day Out

Banhoek in the morning, Boschendal in the afternoon. Two of the best trail hubs in the Western Cape back to back, with lunch at the Boschendal Deli in between. This is the one-day version of the perfect Winelands MTB experience.

📍 Franschhoek Valley 🚵 Intermediate 📅 1 Big Day

Morning

Early start at in the valley. Ride 2-3 hours of world-class singletrack. The valley in the morning light is extraordinary.

Midday

Drive 15 minutes to . Lunch at the Boschendal Deli. One of the best restaurant stops in South Africa — don't rush it.

Afternoon

Ride Boschendal. The Inferno descent is the highlight. Permit R115 at the Trail Centre. End with a coffee or cold drink at the Farm Shop.

Stay

Make a weekend of it and stay in Franschhoek — 10 minutes from both hubs. The town has exceptional restaurants and accommodation from boutique guesthouses to luxury wine estate stays.

Weekend Plan 3

The Durbanville
Family Weekend

Two days of riding across the best family and beginner hubs in the Durbanville network. Great food, wine estate settings and trails that build confidence at every level. Perfect for mixed groups and first-timers.

📍 Durbanville Hills 🟢 Beginner Friendly 👨‍👩‍👧 Families Welcome

Saturday

Morning at — the perfect first MTB experience. Smooth, signed vineyard trails for all abilities. Post-ride lunch at Bon Amis on the estate. One of the best restaurant stops in the Tygerberg network.

Sunday

Morning at for the views. Table Bay on a clear winter morning from the saddle is something you won't forget. Wine tasting at the estate after. Home for Sunday lunch.

Stay

Both hubs are 30 minutes from Cape Town so this works as a day trip from the city. Alternatively Durbanville village has good guesthouses if you want to make a proper weekend of it.
